Minesweeper brings the classic puzzle to Android with custom game modes and a board you can tune to your liking. Play offline, with no ads, tracking or account.

Create modes with your own name, width, height and mine count, or use the included presets. Switch on no-guess mode for boards designed to be solved by logic, or try no-flag mode. Scores are kept separately for different mode settings.

Features:

• Save and resume games in progress
• View recent games and score history
• Choose between reveal and flag controls, with long-press for the other action and optional cording
• Adjust animations, vibration, screen shake, tile display and control behavior
• Pick light or dark mode and different color themes
• Customize the board while keeping the familiar Minesweeper rules

The app is open source and works without network access. It has no ads, analytics, accounts or in-app purchases. If you enjoy it, you can optionally support further development from within the app.
