Minesweeper is a native Android implementation of the classic puzzle game with configurable modes and an offline-first design.

Features:

- Custom board sizes and mine counts
- Optional no-guess mode
- Optional no-flag mode
- Saved in-progress games
- Recent games and score history
- Adjustable controls, animations, vibration, and visual settings
- Light and dark themes

The app does not require network access and does not include ads, analytics, billing, or account features.
